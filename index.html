📜 Recipe Log — Banked & GradedRECIPE GRADE: A−

Entry — the Discord room fix (Nov 13 night): the three pictures, the motion, the monitors. Grade A− as banked: every step verified in the build and the render; the grade rises to A on the Warlord's hand-test inside the Discord room, which is the house verdict.

What was broken: Sheity, The Enity and Verity were the only windows on the floor whose videos carried no still picture behind them, so when the Discord room's frame held their videos back, their windows sat empty while the crew's portraits still showed. The Enity's loop also carried a sound track at 6.8 MB — the first kind of video an embedded frame refuses to play. Nothing on the page started the loops in code, so every desk sat frozen on its first frame, and the desk monitors showed five tiny bars and no reading.

The fix, step for step:

  1. Pictures: pulled one real frame from each leader's own loop (ffmpeg -ss 0.5, libwebp q82) into assets/sheity-poster.webp, assets/enity-poster.webp and assets/verity-poster.webp, and set poster= plus preload="auto" on all five leader video slots — presenter rail (both), the CEO office, the President office, and Verity's wide office. The picture now shows whether or not the loop is playing.
  2. The Enity's loop: re-encoded silent and faststart — ffmpeg -an -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 26 -movflags +faststart — 6.8 MB down to 2.5 MB, same picture, no sound track to refuse.
  3. Motion: one engine (primeVideo / kickMotion) sets muted as a property in code and calls play() on every live loop; it re-fires when the Company screen opens, when the tab comes back, on the first tap, and on a standing 4-second check. Desk videos also carry autoplay + preload="auto". Resting desks (data-live="0") still rest on their portrait frame; reduced-motion still parks all video.
  4. Monitors: monitorHTML now draws a real screen — a DESK SCREEN — LIVE FEED / STANDBY header, nine animated bars, and a readout line of that desk's own feed — and Verity's corner screen reads the board (#737 · RED HOLDS · BULLS 39% · Four Points watcher on post).

Re-apply after any future upgrade (upgrades keep breaking settled things — run this list before calling a build done): ① confirm the five leader slots still hold their poster= attributes and the three poster files are still in assets/; ② if enity-loop.mp4 is ever replaced, re-run step 2's encode on the new file before it ships; ③ confirm the primeVideo/kickMotion block and the desk autoplay attributes survived; ④ confirm monitorHTML still renders the header + readout. Locked offices: the CEO and President offices were NOT touched in layout — only the attributes on their <video> tags changed. Re-apply the same way: attributes only, never the office layout.
